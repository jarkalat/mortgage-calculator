Privacy

Abodex runs in your browser. The one exception is the optional “Explain my results” advisor, described below.

The calculators

There are no accounts, analytics or trackers, and no cookies. Your numbers stay in your browser. Only your language, currency and theme choices are saved there, in your browser’s own storage, and they never leave it.

The “Explain my results” advisor

What is sent, and when

Only when you click “Explain my results”. Then the calculator’s numbers (your inputs and the results shown, such as the loan amount, interest rates and monthly payments) and the page language are sent. Nothing is sent before you click, and nothing else from the page or your browser is included.

Who processes it

Our advisor server runs on Cloudflare. It passes the numbers to Anthropic, which runs the Claude AI that writes the explanation. The explanation then comes back to your browser.

What is stored

  • The explanation, saved under its numbers for 30 days, so that when anyone asks about exactly the same numbers again the answer comes from storage at no cost.
  • A scrambled visitor code (a one-way hash of your connection’s IP address and the current hour), kept for 1 hour to enforce the limit on how many explanations one visitor can ask for.
  • A count of how many explanations the whole site gave each day, to enforce a daily limit. It says nothing about you.
  • Your IP address itself is never stored.

Cookies

The advisor uses no cookies.

About the answers

The explanation is written by AI and can contain mistakes. It is education, not financial advice. Check anything important with your bank or a qualified adviser.
