Yearly table

The numbers behind the chart, at the end of each year (year 0 is the day you buy).

YearBuyer pays / moRent / moHome equityBuyer investedBuyer net worthRenter net worthBuyer minus renter
  • Buyer pays / mo: the mortgage payment plus ownership costs that year.
  • Rent / mo: the renter’s rent that year.
  • Home equity: the home’s value minus the loan left and minus selling costs — what selling would leave you.
  • Buyer invested: the buyer’s savings, built up in months when renting would have cost more.
  • Buyer net worth: home equity plus the buyer’s investments.
  • Renter net worth: the renter’s investments, started with the down payment and buying costs.
  • Buyer minus renter: how far the buyer is ahead (positive) or behind (negative).

Each month, whoever pays less invests the difference. All figures are before tax: taxes on investment gains and on selling a home depend on your country (in Czechia, both can become tax-free after certain holding periods).
