How Abodex works

  • Official data, with sources

    Market figures come from the Czech Statistical Office, the Czech National Bank, the Ministry of Finance and Eurostat, plus Deloitte’s indices where marked as a private source. Each one shows its source and period.

  • Calculations you can check

    Every result comes with a plain-English explanation of what it means, and the mortgage schedule can be downloaded as a CSV file to check line by line.

  • No sign-up, nothing collected

    There are no accounts, cookies, analytics or trackers. Your numbers stay in your browser; only your language, currency and theme choices are saved there.
