## Skyline
`prague-skyline.svg` is the Prague skyline drawing supplied by the site owner (originally `sky.svg`); its shapes are
unchanged, only its fill is `currentColor` and its embedded metadata block was removed. It is inlined in the page HTML
(home hero and every footer) so it can follow the light/dark colours.
